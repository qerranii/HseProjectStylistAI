# AI-Stylist – Your Personal AI Fashion Assistant

AI-Stylist is a smart app designed to help you find your unique style, choose the perfect outfits, and create matching looks with the help of artificial intelligence. Whether you want to experiment with fashion, stay up to date with the latest trends, or get personalized style recommendations, AI-Stylist makes it easy and fun.

## Features of AI-Stylist

- **Personalized Looks based on Location:** On the "For You" page, AI-Stylist generates outfit looks tailored to your current location. As soon as you open this page, your location is detected through a modal window and the app creates looks suitable for your environment and weather conditions. You can save these looks which will then appear in your profile for easy access.

- **Swipe-based Scrolling:** The "Scrolling" feature allows you to browse through a curated feed of outfit looks based on your style preferences. Swipe right to like or left to dislike. The AI learns from your feedback refining future recommendations to better match your tastes. All liked and disliked looks are saved in your profile for review.

- **Community interaction via Posts:** Engage with AI-stylist community by creating posts, commenting and liking other's content. This social feature allows sharing your style, getting inspiration and connecting with other users.

- **User Profile Management:** Your profile page allows you to edit personal information, view saved likes and dislikes, and manage your posts. You can also visit other users' profiles to explore their posts and stylistic contributions.

- **Authentication and Account Management:** The app includes registration, login, and logout functionality to securely manage your account and personalize your experience.

These features combine to create a personalized, interactive, and community-driven stylistic experience that adapts to your preferences and environment.

## Roadmap

- **Location-Based Styling:** Automatically generate outfit recommendations based on user geolocation and local weather conditions on the "For You" page.
- **Swipe Interaction:** Implement swipe right/left to like or dislike outfits; use this feedback to train AI for personalized recommendations.
- **Community Features:** Enable users to create posts, comment, like, and interact within the app’s social feed.
- **Profile Page:** Allow users to edit personal data, view saved liked/disliked outfits, their posts, and visit other users’ profiles.
- **Authentication:** Support user registration, login, and logout for secure personalized experience.
- **Paid Version:** Introduce a subscription model that limits the number of outfit generations in the free version, while offering unlimited outfit generations and premium features to paid users.
- **Mobile Support:** Plan development of mobile apps for iOS and Android.
- **E-Commerce Integration:** Integrate with online stores to allow direct purchase of recommended items.
- **Localization:** Add multilingual support and adapt recommendations to regional fashion trends.
